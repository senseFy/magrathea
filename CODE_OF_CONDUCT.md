# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Magrathea welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, identities, experience
levels, and perspectives. Maintainers, contributors, and community members are expected to make
technical collaboration respectful, safe, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback without attacking the person who produced the work.
- Assume good intent while still naming concrete risks, mistakes, and boundary violations.
- Respect different levels of experience and make project expectations discoverable.
- Protect credentials, private conversation data, vulnerability details, and personal information.
- Accept a maintainer decision after the reasoning and relevant project boundary have been
  documented, while remaining free to propose a later change with new evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal insults, sexualized attention, deliberate misgendering, or repeated unwelcome contact.
- Publishing another person's private information or project-sensitive data without permission.
- Knowingly submitting credentials, private prompts, exploit details, or unrelated personal data.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith misrepresentation, spam, or retaliation against a reporter.

Strong disagreement about architecture, compatibility, testing, or project scope is not itself a
conduct violation. It must remain focused on evidence and behavior rather than identity or motive.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels,
project-controlled communication spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing
Magrathea.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include sensitive conduct details in a public issue. If private vulnerability reporting is
enabled, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/senseFy/magrathea/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Conduct report`. Otherwise, open a public issue containing only a
request to establish a private contact channel.

Include the relevant location and date, a factual description, available links or screenshots, and
any immediate safety concern. Do not collect or disclose unrelated personal information.

Maintainers will limit report access, consider conflicts of interest, gather only the information
needed to evaluate the report, and respond proportionately. Possible actions include a private
correction, content removal, a warning, temporary participation restrictions, or permanent removal
from project spaces. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is itself a
violation.

If a report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict and request review by an uninvolved
maintainer when one is available. Magrathea is an early project and cannot promise a response-time
SLA or an independent appeals body; maintainers will nevertheless document enforcement decisions
to the extent compatible with reporter privacy.
